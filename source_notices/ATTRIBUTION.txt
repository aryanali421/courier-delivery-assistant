Keras CIFAR10 data and class labels: https://keras.io/api/datasets/cifar10/

Tabular scenarios for Projects 02 to 05 and all feedback examples were authored for this class. They are demonstration data, not observations from businesses or field collection.

Image subsets retain the source class labels. They were sampled with seed 42 and resized only where needed. See image_manifest.csv. Train and test sources are kept separate when provided by the source. These small subsets are for classroom study. Keep notices with copies; the public GitHub app needs trained models, not the full image dataset.
VADER lexicon: C. J. Hutto and Eric Gilbert, 2014, distributed through NLTK data. NLTK data source: https://github.com/nltk/nltk_data
